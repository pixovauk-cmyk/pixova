WHY I STARTED PIXOVA

Fed up with agencies that overpromise and underdeliver.

I started Pixova because I kept seeing the same thing. Small businesses paying thousands for a website that looked fine but did nothing. No rankings. No enquiries. Just a nice-looking page nobody ever found.

The problem wasn't the businesses. It was the agencies. Big pitches, complicated proposals, and no one who could explain in plain English what they were actually building or why it would work.

Pixova is different in the way that actually matters: we build websites with SEO from the first line of code, not as an afterthought. Then we manage everything — so you never have to think about it again.

We use AI to build faster and smarter. But every site is reviewed, refined, and backed by a real strategy. Birmingham-based, UK-wide, and always available when you need something sorted.
